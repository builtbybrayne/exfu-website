# First concept review

These three images were generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the supplied business card as a brand reference. They are review mockups, not working pages. The exact prompts are saved alongside them in `image-prompts.json`.

## Split

The closest continuation of the business card. Strong headline and a clearly featured fractional offer. The huge Ex structure and red line are memorable, but the red headline on black and condensed smaller headings need contrast/readability attention. Some generated supplementary copy is generic and should be removed. This version has the strongest direct brand continuity.

## Spatial

The strongest first visual direction. The sculpture gives the page a distinct focal point while the typography remains approachable. The blue is a deliberate new brand extension, not a colour from the card. The rendering flattened the logo's black-white backing, which must be restored from the actual asset in a build. The lower sections are too conventional relative to the hero, and the fractional offer needs more prominence than the defined project. Remove added all-caps filler captions.

## Working canvas

Strong red field and the most concrete link between the visual device and the service. The concept is clearer than it is novel in its current static form. A visitor-driven brief that visibly assembles from their answers could give it more purpose. Avoid repeating the same three-step process immediately below the hero. The generated travel/road images are illustrative placeholders, not project evidence. Remove them or replace them with actual approved project assets.

## Recommendation

Explore Spatial's visual quality with Working canvas's useful brief-building interaction. Preserve ExFu's actual logo and test whether cobalt adds something worth keeping. Give fractional support a larger standalone section and bring a small factual credibility line closer to the hero. Keep the initial page comprehensible before any interaction.

All main headlines and core offer labels are readable in the mockups. Generated secondary filler and logo approximations are not final copy or assets. Before implementation, use the written draft as the copy reference and replace invented decoration with purposeful content.
