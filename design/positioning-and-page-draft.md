# ExFu: positioning and page draft

Working draft for review, 8 September 2026. This is a proposed direction, not approved copy or a built website.

## The commercial decision

Give fractional business enquiries the clearest route. Keep defined projects and individual setup available. Contract and fractional engagements only. Remote work is preferred; Bristol sessions are possible by arrangement. Visitors with an existing brief can contact Alastair directly.

The website should help an unfamiliar visitor understand the work and assess Alastair's judgement. Referrals and a repeat sponsor currently account for most engagements. We have delivery examples, but have not validated this messaging with unfamiliar buyers.

## Positioning hypothesis

ExFu provides fractional AI support and hands-on delivery for leaders who see opportunities for AI and need help choosing, proposing, and implementing them. Alastair can work alongside an existing CTO or internal advocate. An engagement should leave the company with useful work delivered and people able to own what follows.

The strongest candidate for differentiation is the combination of commercial judgement, hands-on engineering, and attention to how people adopt and own the work. The supporting evidence is Alastair's software career, founder experience, psychotherapy training, and recent project decisions and handovers. None of those alone should be presented as unique in the market.

Possible alternatives include the sponsor doing the work themselves, hiring an internal specialist, commissioning a delivery supplier, or continuing experiments without dedicated support. These are hypotheses, not alternatives buyers have confirmed considering.

## Why, how, what

Why: there is worthwhile work a business has not had the capacity to pursue. AI creates opportunities to do some of it. Start from a goal the business cares about, including revenue opportunities.

How: work with the sponsor, investigate the opportunity, test assumptions, make a credible proposal, build with the team, and establish internal ownership. Team involvement and usable interfaces are part of implementation.

What: ongoing fractional support, defined delivery projects, and individual agent setup. The main page prioritises the first. Use the narrative naturally; do not label website sections Why, How, and What.

### The approach

**Give your next AI project a fair chance.**

**Find the opportunity.** Start with a business goal. Look at the work, the people involved, and what would make the result useful.

## Quiz proposal

The visitor's benefit is a useful starting point and a more informed conversation. The quiz should not merely collect contact details.

1. Which situation brings you here? Business-wide AI / A specific project / My own workload.
2. What is the main obstacle? Unsure where to begin / Need to make the case / Need help building / Need people to own it. Adapt wording for the individual route.
3. What would you like to change? Optional short free text, with an option to skip.

Show an immediate, brief suggested starting point, explicitly tentative. Then offer **Send this to Alastair** with name, email, and permission to follow up. Display what will be sent. No account creation.

Do not promise 20 seconds until the actual flow is tested. Treat the budget/pricing-meter interaction as an optional later exploration after the visitor understands the suggested engagement. Do not require several willingness-to-pay answers inside the short starting quiz.

## Supporting pages and search context

Keep a short homepage and link to substantive fractional support, project work, individual support, case-study, experience, and tools pages. Use crawlable text, descriptive headings, concrete examples, consistent naming, and ordinary internal links. Any structured data should describe the visible content accurately. Publish relevant depth for humans and agents alike. No hidden keyword blocks or unsubstantiated claims about guaranteed AI-search placement.

## Visual directions

### 1. Split

Black #000000, white #FFFFFF, brand red #BC3D2B, silver #D8DADC. Heavy condensed grotesk headings with calm sans-serif body. A bold asymmetric black-white division extends the business-card language. A red path connects the hero composition to the invitation. Lower sections alternate open white and black surfaces, with staggered content rather than a repeated card grid.

Potential live behaviour: a visitor-controlled boundary reveals an opportunity and the work needed to pursue it. Keep an immediately readable static default. This interaction would be illustrative, not an automatic assessment.

Strength: closest to the existing brand and strong visual recognition. Risk: a huge sculptural mark can compete with the proposition.

### 2. Spatial

Cool blue #E8F0F5, cobalt #234BE8, black #000000, white #FFFFFF, brand red #BC3D2B. Large humanist sans-serif. A tactile black-white-red paper weave depicts people, tools, and shared context connecting. The rest of the page is spacious and quiet.

Potential live behaviour: selecting a visitor need changes a small set of labelled strands and reveals a relevant example. The text remains usable without animation. Avoid constant ambient motion.

Strength: distinctive hero with room for commercial clarity. Risk: the sculpture could read as tangled work rather than useful connections; test that interpretation.

### 3. Working canvas

Brand red #BC3D2B, black #000000, white #FFFFFF, cool grey #ECEDEF. Wide grotesk headings with readable humanist body. A red hero and arranged paper planes make the progression from question to proposal visible. The quiz can become a meaningful part of the composition.

Potential live behaviour: choices assemble a concise editable opportunity brief. Show what changed and let the visitor send it. This can demonstrate collaboration through an actual useful interaction.

Strength: the strongest opportunity to make the website itself useful. Risk: resembling an agency presentation or making visitors learn an unfamiliar navigation system.

## Design criteria

- A first-time visitor should be able to identify the service and next step from the opening.
- Fractional support is the most prominent offer.
- Boldness belongs in composition and one meaningful interaction. Text and navigation remain readable.
- The logo is anchored to the supplied card. Generated mockups approximate lettering; use the source mark for implementation.
- Mobile layout, keyboard access, contrast, reduced-motion behaviour, and quiz usability require validation in a later implementation.
- The generated images are visual concepts. They do not demonstrate functional agent behaviour or a working website.
- A later short account of the actual design/build collaboration could provide credible evidence of Alastair's agent use. Do not invent a productivity or revenue claim from it.

## Research grounding

[Nielsen Norman Group: Homepage Design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/homepage-design-principles/) supports making purpose, value, and navigation clear. The specific ExFu layouts are design proposals, not conversion-tested findings.

[April Dunford: Positioning and Competition](https://aprildunford.substack.com/p/positioning-and-competition) grounds the distinction between real buyer alternatives and a generic competitor list. ExFu's alternatives still need validation.

[Google: AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) and [Bing: AI Performance](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview) support accessible, well-structured content with substantive information.
