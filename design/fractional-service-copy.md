## When you need more than enthusiasm

You may already have a CTO or someone pushing AI forward. They need time, technical backing, and a proposal leadership can assess. Fractional support adds experience alongside them as the work develops.

We start with a business goal and the assumptions behind it. That might mean exploring a new customer channel, improving access to business knowledge, or building a workflow nobody currently has capacity to run.

## What we can work on

The work can include opportunity assessment, small experiments, leadership proposals, agents, integrations, shared context, and internal tools. Your needs determine the mix of advice, engineering, and team support.

For example, an agent might gather information about potential customers, keep a business tool up to date from existing records, or make knowledge available across teams. Each possibility needs an owner, a useful outcome, and a way to assess whether it works.

Ownership matters from the outset. We involve the people who will use the work and help an internal specialist or team maintain and extend it. Some teams will work directly with agents. Others will benefit from a visible workflow or a familiar interface.

## A first project gives the work somewhere to start

### Choose a goal

Agree what the business wants to achieve and why it matters. Identify who needs to be involved and which assumptions need testing.

### Test the assumptions

Use a small experiment where it can answer an important question. Bring the evidence back to the people deciding what to fund or pursue.

### Build with your team

Develop the useful work and establish who will own it. Agree the scope and working rhythm together. Review what the evidence tells us before deciding the next step.

These are ways to organise the work, not a fixed package or promise that every engagement follows an identical sequence.

## Practical questions

### Can you work alongside our CTO?

Yes. Support can sit alongside your existing technical leadership. We agree who makes decisions, who does the work, and who owns it internally.

### Can we start with a defined project?

Yes. A specific opportunity can be a starting point. We can also discuss ongoing support if you need help across several decisions or pieces of work.

### How are scope and fees agreed?

We discuss the outcome, the work involved, and the level of ongoing support before agreeing an engagement. You can send an initial brief even if the scope is still taking shape.

### Do you work remotely?

Yes. Remote engagements are preferred. In-person work in Bristol can be arranged around the calendar.
