# ExFu website intent

## 1. Why

Help people understand where experienced AI implementation support could make a difference to their work, and start useful conversations with Alastair. Fractional business work is the commercial priority. Visitors may arrive through referrals, previous collaborators, LinkedIn, recruiters, jobs boards, or an agent researching help on their behalf.

The website should itself demonstrate thoughtful human-agent collaboration through the quality and usefulness of the result. Visual novelty serves understanding and personality.

## 2. How

- Speak to a visitor's real situation before introducing services or biography.
- Give fractional business support the clearest route while keeping defined projects and personal setup available.
- Support internal advocates and existing CTOs. Do not imply their replacement.
- Focus on worthwhile work and business outcomes. Revenue opportunities are important, but achieved revenue gains must not be invented.
- Develop internal ownership and team involvement. Treat resistance and usability as implementation concerns.
- Use warm, direct, professional language. No swearing or corporate filler. Ground factual claims in the supplied evidence.
- Make the site bold and distinctive, with calm readable content and room to breathe.
- Provide meaningful depth for both people and agents without visually overwhelming the main journey.
- Distinguish user decisions, design proposals, and verified implementation state. Keep the reasoning legible for future collaborators.

## 3. What / themes

1. Content and enquiries: positioning, service explanations, experience, case evidence, discoverability, and a short needs journey leading to contact.
2. Visual language: a coherent homepage and interior-page system, brand personality, and useful interaction.
3. Later implementation: a functional, accessible website and tested enquiry delivery after the design and implementation plans are agreed.

## 4. Success criteria

- A new visitor understands what ExFu offers and has a clear next action.
- Fractional prospects can assess fit, see relevant experience, and send an existing brief directly.
- A quiz offers a useful tentative starting point and an explicit route to a human follow-up.
- Individual visitors can find personal support without competing equally with the principal business invitation.
- Content provides substantive, consistent facts about the service and its provider.
- Design is recognisably ExFu, with background rhythm, accessible reading contrast, and restrained motion.
- Future agents can distinguish chosen directions from discarded concepts and unresolved options.

## 5. Known user decisions, recorded 2026-09-08

- Fractional and contract work only. Remote preferred; Bristol in person by arrangement.
- Fractional work is financially important and should have the strongest commercial route.
- No niche has been settled; do not invent one.
- The business-card logo is the brand anchor.
- Working canvas (concept 3) is the preferred visual direction. Soften aggressive backgrounds while retaining bold type and accents. Restore some background contrast after an overly pale iteration.
- Keep the name's multiple meanings in a playful section lower down the page.
- Explore the existing avatar fan below the hero. Assess the quiz and pricing meter rather than adopting them unquestioningly.
- Follow ExFu planning and APV principles in this new project.
