# Visual language

Parent: T1-exfu-website, theme 2.

## 1. Why

Per T1 sections 1-2, make a memorable, human-friendly demonstration of ExFu's judgement and agent collaboration. Readers must comfortably assess the service.

## 2. How

- Build from Working canvas: bold grotesk headings, matte paper forms, warm neutrals, restrained red, and the original wordmark.
- Use a pale reading canvas, a perceptible mid-tone putty band, and one warm dark mushroom section. This is the current proposal for restoring contrast; exact colours remain review candidates.
- Reserve deep contrast for a meaningful visual pause. Avoid repeated black-white stripes or full red walls.
- Use generous space between semantic sections. Do not turn every short paragraph into a new screen.
- Homepage: compact paper hero, primary fractional offer with contextual prose, optional avatar recognition, proof, About, lower-page name definitions, and enquiry invitation.
- Interior pages: shorter title block, smaller paper motif, local contents rail, a sustained reading column, specific examples, optional FAQs, and direct contact.
- Keep the actual logo as a source asset for eventual implementation. Raster-generated letterforms are approximations.
- Large body paragraphs remain readable dark text. Extra context must not become tiny low-contrast footnotes.
- Any interaction must have an intelligible static state, reduced-motion option, and keyboard/touch equivalents.

## 3. What

Review a homepage/service image pair. Assess visual rhythm, fractional emphasis, avatar placement, reading density, and brand continuity. Images are design evidence, not functional UI verification. Preserve prompt files and image versions.

## 4. Open questions (HITL)

- **Q1:** Is the warm dark section the right amount of contrast?
- **Q2:** Does the avatar fan help recognition or distract from the offer?
- **Q3:** Does the interior page retain enough personality while supporting a longer explanation?
